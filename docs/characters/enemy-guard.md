# 敵：Guard

- Moves more slowly and blocks ordinary pressure
- Uses a braced defensive posture with a crude readable shield
- Rewards guard breaks, strong Techniques, or better positioning
- Must read as a Guard through shield, shoulders, and planted stance before labels
- Does not use the Rusher's lunging posture or the Skirmisher's ranged posture
- Represents the ordinary enemy role rather than the named target 鐘なき騎士

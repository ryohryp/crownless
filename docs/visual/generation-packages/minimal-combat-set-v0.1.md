## Request

Create the smallest fixed-oblique combat set: an unarmed player; ordinary
Rusher, Guard, and Skirmisher enemies; a dropped sword and dagger; black-ink
slash, impact, and recoil marks; and muted-vermilion arc, circle, and
directional telegraphs. Keep every element immediately readable on a phone and
exclude UI, text, logos, control hints, loot beams, and glowing VFX.

## Style lock

Use Crownless's living medieval manuscript and rough woodcut grammar: irregular
hand-inked contours, crosshatched shadow, restrained parchment / ash / earth
color, weathered asymmetry, compact non-chibi figures, small restrained
folk-art faces, physical black ink for action, and muted vermilion for danger.

Avoid photorealism, painterly or realistic concept art, AAA 3D, modern chibi,
anime-gacha figures, clean vector cartoons, generic Diablo imitation, glossy
mobile-RPG presentation, neon magic, rarity colors, and particle clutter.

## Subject locks

- Player: unknown, intentionally unarmed, wrapped emphasized hands, patched
  clothing and scavenged pouches, no crown, class costume, shield, or weapon.
- Rusher: hunched and forward-driving close-pressure silhouette, no shield or
  ranged posture, not a named hunt target.
- Guard: planted posture behind a broad crude shield with a weathered side
  weapon, not a polished heroic knight or named hunt target.
- Skirmisher: narrow retreat-ready posture with a crude short bow and readable
  aim direction, not a shield wall, rusher, or named hunt target.

## Review result

The eight files registered in
`assets/combat/minimal-v0.1/README.md` passed role, transparency, and visual
review as prototype candidates. The first Guard and dagger attempts were
rejected for role / subject ambiguity; their replacements were generated again
from the Approved Anchors. None of these candidates has been promoted to an
Approved Visual Anchor.

# Direct discovery playtest

Use this after the #55 exploration simplification lands.

1. From the Grey Hearth, start an expedition.
2. Confirm meaningful destination choices are visible immediately; there is no fog-cell reveal or travel step first.
3. Confirm no more than three destinations are presented at once.
4. Enter each kind of available destination directly and confirm existing encounter/event/dungeon behavior still starts.
5. Win an encounter, collect unsecured loot, and continue. Confirm the next destination choice is direct again.
6. Confirm the continue-versus-return decision still exposes unsecured loot tension.
7. Return to the Hearth and confirm secured loot, Renown, hunts, dungeon progress, and save behavior remain intact.
8. Repeat on a phone-sized viewport and count non-meaningful taps between Hearth and encounter. Target: none beyond starting the expedition and choosing the destination.

The map is no longer a mandatory traversal mechanic. Future world-map presentation should visualize discovery/progress without adding routine travel interactions.
